# Football project preferences

The user excludes **BTTS No** from all future suggested selections. For BTTS markets, consider **BTTS Yes only**, including inside builders. This is the user's market preference, not evidence that either side of the market is universally better. Do not automatically replace an excluded No with Yes; Yes requires its own evidence. Preserve historical recommendations, including excluded markets, for honest outcome review.

Separate research/watchlist outputs from final selections. Do not describe unresolved candidates as the main ticket. Keep the original timestamped decision and record later changes separately. New matchday chats should use the saved reports and these preferences, not assume that prior winning tickets validate the method.

Include candidates, important passes/reserves and zero-final-selection slates. Preserve the original report and capture time; retrospective imports are not prospective forecasts. Do not turn the international results or the earlier three winning groups into fixed numerical feature weights or a guaranteed league-football edge.
