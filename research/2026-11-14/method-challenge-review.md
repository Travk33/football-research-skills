## Portugal

- **What survives challenge.** Portugal had a meaningful raw volume advantage at home in both games and in the away reverse against Denmark. The home 42–14 attempts and 12–3 shots on target came from just Wales and Norway. Wales contributed 23–5 attempts and 5–0 on target, producing a 1–0 win. The reverse produced 19–12 and 10–3 with four Portugal goals, but Denmark equalized twice and Cancelo scored in combination with Ramos. Leaving out the reverse still shows three wins and 53–34 attempts, 16–9 on target. Isolating the remaining Norway pair after also setting aside Wales gives **30–29 attempts and 11–9 on target**, with both wins by one goal. This sensitivity identifies concentration of evidence while retaining the full sample. [Recent competitive ledger](recent-competitive-ledger.json), [UEFA reports ledger](official-match-statistics.json), [independent review](final-independent-review.md).
- **Where the home evidence transfers poorly.** Norway is a useful qualitative check. At the same away venue Denmark drew the shot counts 13–13 and 7–7 on target in a defeat, while Portugal won from 11–20 and 4–6. The Wales match shows Portugal constrained Wales on that date. A two-game home split with three total shots on target conceded does not establish comparable suppression of Denmark's runs behind the defensive line. The saved observations have no common xG, non-penalty xG, or verified chance-location breakdown, and most incident fields are incomplete. Counts cannot tell whether the next shot mix, creation while level, finishing, or defensive exposure will resemble the reverse. [Recent competitive ledger](recent-competitive-ledger.json), [UEFA reports ledger](official-match-statistics.json).
- **What lineup and table updates could resolve.** Nuno Mendes and João Cancelo were already ruled out, and the loss of Leão and Francisco Conceição reduced the wing options. The missing roles matter on both sides of the ball. The XI can name the replacements and the players expected to provide width and cover, but repeating the attacking configuration or safely defending Damsgaard-to-Højlund runs requires evidence the roles work together. Portugal had already qualified and a point sealed first place. Denmark's coach considered a draw useful, with the earlier Norway–Wales result a material input. The branch to assess is what both teams have to gain by pursuing the win when level late. The report flagged this and called for a recheck, while the structured Portugal blockers omitted the incentive update. A fresh quote and even the best plausible XI at the same price could leave the overall case insufficient. Gyökeres or Isak with fit creators improves the plausible attacking plan, particularly after October withdrawals, but starting status alone cannot establish Kulusevski's usable minutes, chance quality against the block, or a reliable winning margin. ## What to carry into club matchdays

The latest four are nested in the last eight and include a shared reverse fixture. The older games are useful checks on interpretation, with World Cup neutral and co-host venues, different opponents, mixed competition stages and incompletely verified coaching and player-role comparability. No older match supplied comparable advanced metrics. Retain those divisions across leagues and cups, especially when a promotion, manager change, rotation or aggregate score alters the meaning of prior results. The original source reconciliation, consistent UEFA counts, regulation-time check, preserved passes and empty final table are worth retaining. [Recent competitive ledger](recent-competitive-ledger.json), [competition context](../../skills/football-wins-btts/references/competition-context.md), [pre-match audit](report.md#audit-models-and-remaining-checks).

1. **Test the strongest supporting claim on matched evidence.** Use the same directional test for both sides where coverage permits, and retain venue, score state, lineup, penalties and dismissals where they affect the comparison. Inspect verified non-penalty chance quality first. When the main source lacks it, try a direct match-details route for the decision-critical games, checking date, teams, venue and score before taking the same provider's comparable values. Preserve count disagreements. When quality estimates remain unavailable, use documented chance locations, big chances or decisive sequences where comparable. If the remaining evidence is raw volume, record which part of the football conclusion it can support and which part remains unestablished. Give a shared reverse one observation's worth of weight.
2. **Run the favorable-XI counterfactual before marking a candidate conditional.** State what a specific lineup or news update would resolve, then ask whether the favorable case at the same terms would leave a decision-critical issue open. Use a price pass with a concrete reason to revisit when a major comparative, tactical, incentive or price objection survives. When the XI arrives, reassess the football case and verify the current quote and contract before changing the status.
3. **Make the decision visible at the point of display.** Lead the slate and chat delivery with the final selections and state the observed-terms verdict beside every watchlist row. Use rank only for a stated purpose and include the decisive evidence that would change the status. Record each side's likely late-level incentives where they affect a winner or a team scoring route. Apply the same price judgment to BTTS Yes and keep its two scoring routes separate. Denmark's credible route alone did not justify the separately quoted BTTS Yes alternative.
4. **Evaluate this change prospectively.** At the first club slate, save the reason and evidence required to reopen every close pass before kickoff. At the first few completed club slates, audit all retained candidates and near passes for whether the late review cleared the stated uncertainty and whether the displayed statuses matched the ledger. Judge this initially as a process check. An eventual hit-rate or calibration comparison needs a defined prospective sample and recorded forecasts.
