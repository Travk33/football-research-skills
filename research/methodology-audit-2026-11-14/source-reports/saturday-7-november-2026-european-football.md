## What decided the selections

For a win, I examined whether the favourite consistently creates better chances than it allows, whether the opponent's defence concedes those chances, and how often the opponent can threaten at the relevant venue. For BTTS, each team's route to a goal must stand up separately. I compared season results with the most recent five or six league matches where available, then tested the story against home or away samples. Expected goals (xG) measure shot quality. Shots on target add information about volume. The two measures overlap, so agreement strengthens the interpretation without doubling the sample size.

League games supplied the baseline. European and cup games informed recovery, squad availability and game context. A red card early in a match can affect the next 80 minutes of shot data. A five-yellow suspension matters when it removes someone from the next starting lineup. Head-to-head streaks, raw card totals and a projected XI carried little weight by themselves. The public datasets vary in coverage and xG definition. Each numerical comparison below uses the same provider for both teams.
