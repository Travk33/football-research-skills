## What has improved in the method

The recent winning results are encouraging, but a few successful slates cannot establish a durable advantage. A close win is not automatically a poor prediction: what matters is the chance quality, game state and price available before kickoff. The same standard applies to a narrow loss. The research therefore starts again from each matchup's evidence rather than carrying forward confidence from previous winners.

| Evidence | How it informs this assessment | How overconfidence is avoided |
|---|---|---|
| Chance quality and volume | Season xG/xGA, recent five-game samples, shots and shots on target | Same provider within each numerical comparison; source disagreements are disclosed. |
| Venue and opposition | Compare the home attack with the away defence, and each opponent's scoring route | Four or five venue games are a small sample. Opponent quality is considered qualitatively; there is no fitted opponent-adjustment model here. |
| Finishing and goalkeeping | Compare actual goals with underlying chances | A run of clean sheets or five-goal wins can overstate repeatable strength. |
| Playing styles | Pressing, territorial pressure, defensive shape, set pieces and transitions | A tactical advantage needs a credible mechanism. Pressing intensity is not proof that an opponent cannot escape it. |
| Duels and wide matchups | Check defensive absences, side of the pitch, supporting runners and defensive cover | Reliable paired player duel data were not available across the slate. No individual success percentages or invented winger/fullback mismatches are supplied. |
| Cards and game state | Confirm competition-specific bans; discount red-card-distorted performances | A referee's card rate alone does not predict a dismissal. A European ban is not automatically a domestic ban. |
| Team news and recovery | Prefer fresh official updates; examine Thursday travel, minutes and rotation | A predicted XI is not a confirmed XI. Returned players may strengthen defence as well as attack. |
| Head-to-head | Use recent meetings as context where coaches and personnel remain comparable | Long historical streaks do not outweigh current chance creation or availability. |
| Price | Compare the required success rate with the strength and uncertainty of the case | A plausible outcome is not necessarily a worthwhile bet; no arbitrary “fair odds” are invented. |

For BTTS, both scoring routes must survive independently. “Both concede often” is insufficient if one attack creates very little. For wins, a weak opponent is insufficient if the favourite's attack is depleted or the odds require an implausibly high level of certainty. Tactical evidence is used to explain the existing numbers and test them, not counted as another independent statistical sample.

### Data discipline and tomorrow's lineup checks

- Do not merge raw xG from different providers into one average. OddAlerts aggregates and Xgoals match ledgers differ materially for some Bournemouth games; they are separate signals, not interchangeable numbers.
- Season/league/venue/all-competition samples are explicitly labelled. Four home/away games should be shrunk toward a broader baseline, not treated as a definitive win probability.
- No reliable paired winger-versus-fullback duel attempts, duel success rates, dribbled-past rates or possession-adjusted defensive data were verified. Naming a supposed individual mismatch without them would be invented precision.
- Brighton's pressing and set-piece numbers are unusually useful verified tactical evidence. That still does not prove Brentford are specifically vulnerable to those mechanisms.
- Published model percentages have not been averaged or repackaged as our own calibrated model. The report contains evidence-based judgements and price hurdles, not fabricated certainty.
- Promotion qualification must be checked on the actual bet. A team leading by two can settle a qualifying win market under the user's offer; it does not settle BTTS, and promotional settlement is not the same thing as a full-time win.

The strongest improvement over simply celebrating previous winners is to retain these passes, record the price and reasoning before kickoff, and score predictions against ordinary results and qualifying early payouts separately.
