## A 44-fixture Saturday

1. **Verify and account for the slate.** Build the fixture inventory from official schedules across the requested competitions, with date and Europe/London kickoffs, actual venue and status. Reconcile the expected count and record excluded or unverified fixtures with the reason. Keep one row per fixture and a separate initial disposition for each in-scope market.
2. **Give both markets a fair first pass.** For a winner, check for a venue-aware strength gap and an obvious draw or opponent scoring threat. For BTTS Yes, check whether each side has shown recent scoring or chance creation against comparable opponents and whether the opposing defence leaves a plausible route. Record decisive availability and available price or quote status. A short favourite price, high match total or data-poor league is an unreliable screen on its own. Keep missing evidence distinct from an adverse finding and record what would reopen a borderline case.
3. **Deepen the plausible or reversible cases.** For both teams, pull the broader baseline and recent match-level results, same-provider chance data where comparable, and relevant incident and lineup context. Ask which supported mechanism favours the selection, what the opponent can do, and whether an early lead, compact block or material XI change breaks the case. Check the closest counterexample and whether dropping the most favourable match changes the conclusion. Where xG is unavailable, verified shots, shot locations or match reports can support a narrower claim. Preserve the gap in chance-quality evidence and pass where it is decisive.
4. **Compare finalists on comparable evidence and actual terms.** Where both win and BTTS Yes are plausible in one game, compare them at contemporaneous quotes, with their different failure modes. A dominant favourite and a visitor with one thin scoring route can support the win while leaving BTTS Yes unresolved. Order the researched cases by the strength and completeness of their evidence and explain close calls. Report the actual counts screened, researched in depth, pending and unverified. A slate-wide probability ranking would require comparable evidence and a defensible forecast across the slate.
5. **Refresh and publish the decision.** Check exact bookmaker, region, market and settlement, fresh price, and any XI condition material to the original case. A missing quote remains quote pending. Record time and reason for each promotion or downgrade and keep open conditions in the watchlist. Let the number of completed selections follow the evidence. Record original decisions before kickoff.
