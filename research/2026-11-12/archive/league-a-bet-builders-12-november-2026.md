## How the shortlist was selected

The most useful order for player props is: exact market definition; expected role and minutes; game-by-game event counts at the offered threshold; opponent opportunity; separate club corroboration; current availability; price and shared failure modes. Team xG and possession describe the game environment, but neither proves a named player will foul, tackle or hit the target. Raw duel-win percentages do not supply the missing event volume.

The research screened shots, shots on target, fouls committed, fouls drawn and tackles across these four games. It did not build a calibrated forecast for every quoted player. The ordering above is not a ranking of precise win probabilities. Four international matches are a small sample, and two of Olise's observations are substitute appearances. Club and international records remain separate.

## Settlement and pricing checks

- SOT is not every attempt in the direction of goal: ordinary blocks and woodwork that stays out do not qualify. Tackles require the provider's ground-challenge event, not every duel, interception or ball recovery. A foul committed must generate a recorded free-kick or penalty event; a later caution after advantage does not necessarily add one. The current bet365 rules state that pre-match selected-player bets are void if the player does not start, subject to the actual contract and relevant exceptions. [bet365 statistics rules](https://help.bet365.com/s/en/sportsrules/soccer/match-statistics).
- The relevant markets displayed **Sub On Play On**. Check eligibility and the replacement rules on the actual slip. Replacement protection changes settlement, but it does not establish the replacement's role or event rate. [bet365 explanation](https://news.bet365.com/en-gb/article/what-happens-if-a-footballer-i-bet-on-gets-injured/2026012011231909727).
- A two-goal early payout relates to an eligible result leg. It does not make a player prop, corner leg or entire builder win. [Early payout terms](https://www.bet365.com/promos/en/home/early-payout-offer).
- Reciprocal decimal odds are the break-even hurdle for an ordinary binary bet, not our predicted probability. At 1.25 the hurdle is 80%; at 1.20 it is 83.3%. A three- or four-match streak is insufficient to establish those true probabilities. No fair-price threshold is invented here.
- Combining all four pairs would require eight events. The three quoted pairs also have no verified combined price. Selecting every researched match is not part of the methodology.
