## What changed in the method

1. **Choose the football proposition before chasing a payout.** Strong possession can support territorial pressure without establishing a win, clean sheet, corner total or named player's shot count. Each market needs its own evidence.
2. **Use comparable recent international matches.** The four current Nations League games are the most comparable core. Older competitive results add context; friendlies, club performances and neutral World Cup fixtures are not pooled as interchangeable home/away observations.
3. **Separate penalties, red cards, finishing and own goals.** An early dismissal can invalidate a normal 11-v-11 comparison. A 7–0 result can dominate an average. A penalty can make otherwise limited creation look much stronger.
4. **Check both sides and the actual venue.** For corners, inspect both a team's corners won and the opponent's concessions. For goals, inspect both scoring routes. Some CONCACAF nominal away teams are the actual hosts.
5. **Use player role and minutes before per-90 rates.** Two successful substitute appearances do not establish a dependable starting-player rate. Changes in teammates can also change shot share, pressing duties and set pieces.
6. **Distinguish observations from tactical inference.** Box touches and shot locations are observed. A prediction about a full-back being isolated is a hypothesis until personnel and positioning support it. Duel wins alone do not establish betting value.
7. **Compare the exact contract and price.** Ordinary win, two-goal early payout, Asian handicap, European handicap and DNB settle differently. A lower line can cost so much that it becomes unattractive.
8. **Try to disprove the shortlist.** Check outliers, duplicate reverse fixtures, threshold sensitivity and the risk of finding attractive streaks simply because many markets were searched.

There is no complete, calibrated last-ten-match model for every team and every prop here. The detailed refreshed match metrics below come from FotMob's displayed match statistics. The earlier report used other providers in places; these are not blended into a single xG series. Missing data remain missing.
