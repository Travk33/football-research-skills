## What to learn, without overfitting

- **Keep the selection ledger's status field.** England win/Montenegro win were core conditional candidates; Albania outright was a watch; Italy–France BTTS Yes was a price pass; the Czechia–Spain pair was only a watch. Count user-reported tickets separately from researched directions and market outcomes.
- **Corners:** England's four observed totals were exactly six. The 6+ threshold was a thin-margin pattern, not evidence of a comfortable cushion. Record the actual match total before deciding whether this was a useful signal.
- **Possession and tackles:** Cucurella played 90 minutes, made 0 tackles, and Spain had 70% possession. Czechia's listed starting right-sided attacking-midfielder in its 4-2-3-1 was Lukáš Ambros (76 minutes); his current FotMob row has 0 successful dribbles and 2 times dribbled past. The feed has no carries volume, so it cannot substantiate a specific amount of wing pressure or prove a direct Cucurella–Ambros matchup. Czechia's red card at 69' is an additional game-state confounder. Root review of the first-half data shows Spain already had 67% possession and Czechia only one shot (0.02 xG) before half-time. Cucurella contested only one ground duel over the match, won none, and recorded one block, two clearances and one interception; those are not tackles. This supports an exposure explanation without proving a particular winger caused the outcome. The outcome supports recording possession and opponent-side activity as explicit tackle checks; it does not establish that one winger-quality explanation alone caused the miss.
- **Player props settle independently.** FotMob recorded Güler at 3 SOT, Çelik at 2 tackles, Olise at 1 SOT, Tonali at 1 foul, Anderson at 1 foul, Vušković at 1 foul, Hložek at 1 shot and Cucurella at 0 tackles. A goal ordinarily counts as a shot on target, absent a rare later attribution correction; these counts now verify the event outcomes directly. Çelik's counted tackles do not decide whether a non-starter selection is void under the user's specific bookmaker contract.
- **Use the exact line and price.** The under-3.5 and displayed Under-4 labels require their own exact contracts; DNB is not an outright win market; and a standalone price is not a same-game builder price. Keep the original quote beside the result.
- **No track-record conclusion yet.** Match event data confirms the legs' statistical outcomes, not the user's actual slip composition or bookmaker settlement. The user reports the initial win+BTTS ticket won and the builder lost; retain those as user-reported placements/settlements unless the slip or operator settlement record is available.

## Settlement-rule refresh, 13 November morning

bet365's current [match-statistics rules](https://help.bet365.com/s/en/sportsrules/soccer/match-statistics) say player statistics selections are void when the selected player does not start, apart from specified rolling-substitution competitions. On those standard terms, Çelik's pre-match tackle selection would be void even though he recorded two tackles after coming on. The user's exact final ticket was not inspected; its recalculated return is not established here. It does not establish that a selected non-starter is an ordinary winning leg.
