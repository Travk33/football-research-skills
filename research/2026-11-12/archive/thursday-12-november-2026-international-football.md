## Method adapted for international football

1. **Verify fixture, actual venue and competition.** Nominal home/away ordering can mislead in centralized CONCACAF windows and neutral CAF venues. Separate senior A teams from youth, local-player and second-string teams.
2. **Start with repeatable chance creation and prevention.** Use same-provider xG, non-penalty xG when available, shots inside the box, shots on target and opponent scoring routes. Raw possession, total tackles and goals alone are weaker evidence.
3. **Keep the sample honest.** The current Nations League campaign has four games per team; the AFCON groups have only two. Older competitive games and World Cup matches give context, but different opponents, coaches and venues prevent treating them as an interchangeable ten-game sample.
4. **Adjust for game state.** A 22nd-minute goalkeeper dismissal, penalties, finishing outliers and late score changes can distort the apparent strength of a win or defeat. A team under pressure often makes more tackles; that does not automatically mean better defending.
5. **Check current squads and incentives.** Club minutes help assess readiness, but club attacking output is not the national team's scoring rate. New coaches, travel, rotation, qualification status and the next international fixture matter.
6. **Use tactics as a testable mechanism.** Assess how width, transitions, set pieces and midfield cover could create chances. No verified comprehensive player-duel or tracking dataset was available, so named winger/full-back mismatches and precise duel percentages are not invented.
7. **For BTTS, require two credible scoring routes.** One weak attack can invalidate an apparently open matchup. Both teams conceding recently is not enough. Recent head-to-head has context value; old national-team reputations have little predictive force after personnel changes.
8. **Evaluate the exact market and price.** A likely winner can be too short; a higher price can still be poor. Referee/card context is useful only with verified appointments and relevant evidence. No numerical model edge, referee dismissal probability or two-goal-payout uplift is claimed.

The leading candidates therefore remain conditional on Thursday's starting teams and late news. This is the completed Tuesday research assessment; it does not assume any automatic monitoring or future update.
