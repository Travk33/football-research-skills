## Import procedure

1. Label records historical. Forecasts, original method/model versions and bookmaker settlement remain null/unknown unless evidenced. Do not fabricate pre-match publication timestamps.
2. If original reports are available in the user's workspace, read complete relevant sections to recover evidence and counterarguments. They are not automatically present in a fresh public clone.
3. If absent, render honest inventory summaries with a missing-detail label. Do not recreate old reasoning from current results. Report labels must not become broken public download links.
4. Copy only sanitized research facts and source links, never the surrounding chat or account screenshots.
5. Verify results separately. Retain documented status until fresh verification succeeds, with corrections and conflicts preserved.
6. Add prospective slates from genuinely recorded future decisions; keep historical and prospective performance separate.
