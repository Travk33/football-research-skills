# Other markets — read first

**Purpose:** assess match/team totals, handicaps, corners and cards at exact available lines.

**Process:** reuse verified team evidence → identify plausible markets → examine exact thresholds → test tactical/game-state mechanisms → compare alternatives → check price/rules → journal.

- **Goals:** both attacks against both defences; chance quality, availability and goal-distribution uncertainty.
- **Handicaps:** winning margin, opponent resistance and precise Asian/European settlement.
- **Corners:** production/concessions, width, blocked deliveries and early-lead scenarios. Dominance alone is insufficient.
- **Cards:** contact exposure, team/player roles and comparable referee evidence.

Retain match-by-match hits, misses and unknowns. Compare a preferred alternative directly with the relevant win/BTTS case; a higher price alone does not justify switching markets.

**Output:** exact selection/line, sourced counts, tactical explanation, strongest counterargument, current odds/time, settlement terms and status. Keep unresolved cases outside the final table.
