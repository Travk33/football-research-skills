# Bet builders — read first

**Purpose:** investigate two compatible selections per specified fixture; a third needs separate support. One watch or no builder is a valid result. The user's normal daily scope is at most five fixtures.

**Process:** verify available markets → collect player ledgers → check roles/minutes → test opponent exposure → assess each leg → examine dependence → obtain actual joint quote → journal.

- Keep starts, substitutions, minutes, zeros and missing counts separate; distinguish club/international samples and ordinary-time events.
- Connect shots to shooting opportunities, fouls to defensive contact, fouls drawn to receiving/carrying, and tackles to controlled opposition attacks into the player's zone.
- Check both teams' tactics, current roles, substitution risk and early-lead scenarios. Historical tackle counts need a credible opportunity mechanism.
- Compare adjacent thresholds and preserve failures. A strong first leg does not justify a weak second leg.

**Output:** detailed evidence for each leg, joint failure scenarios, individual prices and the **actual combined quote**, verified rules and status. Unresolved material role/contract/quote checks prevent final promotion. Follow project exclusions, including BTTS No in AI-Bets.

**Roles:** cheaper workers retrieve; the selected lead owns tactical interpretation and the final combination. See [model routing](references/model-routing.md).
