# Player market mechanisms

## Shots and shots on target

Use total attempts, SOT, shot location/type and player share of team attempts. Include blocked and off-target shots for total-shot markets under the actual bookmaker definition. Do not count every shot-map flag as SOT: providers may attach on-target information to blocked attempts differently. Prefer the provider's explicit statistic and reconcile inconsistencies.

Minutes and role can dominate a shooting average. Separate starts from substitute appearances; report both rather than select the more favorable sample. Confirm free-kick and penalty duty rather than infer it from reputation. A teammate's absence can shift shot share while reducing total team creation.

Compare 1+ versus 2+ SOT directly. Several one-SOT games support a different contract from two or more. A 4/4 recent appearance record with two short substitute successes is descriptive evidence, not a 100% forecast for a start. Preserve club misses even when the national sample is perfect.

Check woodwork, ordinary blocks, last-line blocks and own-goal definitions in current bookmaker rules. Do not transfer a quote from a bookmaker with different event definitions without identifying the difference.

## Fouls committed

Look for repeated role-specific contact: midfield recovery, pressing, covering an advancing full-back, stopping transitions or defending a dribbler. Record actual fouls and minutes, not just yellow cards or duel attempts. Verify whether advantage incidents count; a later caution can be separate from the provider's foul event.

Opponent dribble volume can create exposure but does not guarantee a foul. A defender can win cleanly, be bypassed, drop off or receive cover. Distinguish the plausible zone matchup from a confirmed man-marking instruction. Referee context should be competition-comparable where possible.

Check higher thresholds against both recent and broader samples. One foul in every match can coexist with very few two-foul games. A short price on 1+ does not justify automatically selecting 2+.

## To be fouled

Combine actual fouls drawn with touches/receipts under pressure, carries, dribble attempts and expected opponent contact. A winger with little possession may not generate many incidents despite a favorable-looking defender. Positional changes and set-piece-heavy attacking roles can change exposure.

For a pairing with an opponent to commit a foul, disclose shared-incident dependence. A player can be fouled by several different opponents; do not imply one exact defender must provide it.

## Tackles

Verify total tackle events versus tackles won, attempted tackles, ground duels, recoveries and interceptions. These are not interchangeable. The bookmaker's settlement provider defines the event; check current terms and whether the research feed's statistic maps to it.

Look for controlled-possession opposition attacks through the player's zone, role/position, cover, likely time defending and successful ground challenges. Long balls, aerial contests and passive containment may generate pressure without recorded tackles. Possession dominance can reduce a favorite's tackle opportunities.

For the opportunity case, record the likely opponent/zone, available evidence of carries or take-ons there, whether attacks arrive on the ground, and the defender's cover or pressing role. If location-level data are unavailable, say so and lower confidence; do not manufacture a winger matchup. Check a low-exposure scenario explicitly: an early lead, an opponent retreating, a teammate making the challenge, or a direct-ball opponent bypassing the defender. High possession is a risk factor, not a universal veto: an opponent with verified transition volume may still generate tackles.

In post-match reviews, separate event types and game states. One ground duel does not imply one tackle, and clearances, blocks or recoveries cannot rescue a tackle threshold. Check red-card timing and, where available, pre-red possession/activity before attributing the result to full-match possession. A zero-tackle result alone does not prove that the opponent's winger lacked ability.

Inspect exact counts near the line. A sequence 2,2,5,2 is materially different at 2+ and 3+. Club evidence can challenge a national streak. A new winger opponent or formation can change the exposure; label the inference and retain an adverse scenario.

## Cards, assists and goals as builder legs

Cards need their own event and referee analysis; fouls alone are insufficient. Assists depend on the recipient finishing and the provider's final-touch definition. Goals need chance quality/volume and role, not simply a recent scoring streak. Do not add these more variable events merely because they make the payout attractive.

## Pair selection

Prefer two separately supported legs with a coherent joint scenario, not two arbitrary short prices. State whether the pair relies on sustained possession, a competitive scoreline, opposing transitions or sufficient minutes. A two-leg combination can fail through either leg and should not inherit the confidence of its strongest component.
