# Evidence quality and decision-changing uncertainty

Use these checks for finalists and borderline exclusions. They guide judgment; they are not fitted numerical weights.

## Weight evidence by what it can establish

For match winners and goal markets, comparable chance creation/prevention is primary: verified xG/npxG where available, shot quality/location, repeatable open-play and set-piece routes, and the opposing attack. Results and shots/SOT provide context but cannot substitute for chance quality. A side can lose the shot count while creating the better chances. For player events, corners and cards, the relevant event-count distribution and opportunity mechanism are primary; team xG is context rather than a replacement.

If decisive chance-quality evidence is missing, try a credible direct match-details/provider route for the key games and document access attempts. Verify teams, date, venue, period and score before using retrieved history. Official score/discipline sources need not contain xG; identify the separate statistics provider. Do not label data globally unavailable merely because the first feed omits it. Do not scrape indefinitely: retain the gap and narrow/pass the claim if credible alternatives fail.

Use the same provider inside numerical comparisons. Keep source disagreements visible, including SOT classification and retrospective corrections. Data fetched after kickoff may diagnose an omission; it must not be presented as something used in the original decision or as proof of what a historical endpoint exposed then.

## Test support symmetrically

Apply comparable opponent and incident tests to both teams. If discounting a weak opponent or penalty-inflated result for one team, check the equivalent issue for the other. Show the full sample plus clearly defined sensitivity; do not search for a favorable window. Ask whether the advantage depends mainly on one reverse meeting, weak opponent, venue or isolated hot finish. A shared reverse is one match, and a recent subset is not independent of the broader sample.

Two venue games do not establish a stable home/away effect. Compare the closest credible opponent contexts and state remaining mismatches rather than invent a numerical adjustment.

## Make context alter the decision

Check managerial regime, actual roles and availability, opponent style, rest/travel, and match incentives. A missing player changes more than a name: assess replacement supply, cover and interactions. Compare the expected current lineup with the lineup that produced the favorable baseline. Do not transfer a functioning old full-back/winger combination unchanged to new personnel.

For winner markets, test the draw route even when the opponent has limited scoring. When a point benefits either team, state the verified table arithmetic and the late-level scenario; refresh after relevant earlier results. Incentives are scenario evidence, not a fixed percentage deduction or proof of reduced effort.

Use goalkeeper evidence to distinguish chance prevention, shot stopping and finishing variation. One unusual xGOT or save performance does not establish permanent ability. Use referee/contact evidence where it changes the exact card/foul or event case; do not pad a winner rationale with an unrelated card average. Duels need attempts, location, role and opponent context. Do not invent exact marking assignments or claim a full-back caused a goal without supporting event/location evidence.

## Separate an open check from a weak case

Before assigning conditional status, assume the most favorable plausible XI and that the quote is executable. Would a material evidence, tactical, incentive or price objection still prevent selection? If so, retain a reserve/pass or insufficient-evidence decision with a substantive reopening requirement. A confirmed team sheet is not a cure for that objection.

Name what the pending check can actually resolve. Publish final selections first, then research-only rows with the observed-terms verdict and explicit remaining blockers. Rank only what was comparably investigated. Strong justification includes decisive supporting counts, the strongest opposing observation, tactical inference and why the opposing evidence changes or does not change the decision.

## Prospective evaluation

Track whether these checks were executed across the next four completed research slates, including ordinary league football. Review all finalists and close passes, not only losses. Initial success criteria are source coverage, symmetric comparisons, substantive conditions and accurate status communication. Forecast accuracy/value requires a larger predefined prospective sample and genuinely recorded probabilities/prices; do not fit numerical feature weights from three winning tickets or one international loss.
